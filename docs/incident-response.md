# Incident Response

## Severity Levels
- SEV1: Live trading risk or financial loss.
- SEV2: Broker connectivity or data integrity issues.
- SEV3: Non-critical UI/ops disruptions.

## Immediate Actions
1. Trigger panic stop if safety is uncertain.
2. Pause trading for investigation.
3. Preserve logs and broker evidence.

## Communication
- Notify on-call and stakeholders.
- Use a single incident channel to reduce noise.

## Recovery
- Reconcile broker state.
- Validate positions/orders.
- Resume only after explicit approval.

## Post-Incident
- Complete postmortem within 48 hours.
- Track corrective actions.
